# Brief: [document title]

*Target: ~5 minutes of Mike's time. The agent pre-fills sections 1–4 from the materials; Mike fills 5–8 (bullets are fine, fragments are fine). Anything left blank, the agent flags rather than invents.*

## 1. The document (agent fills)

What it is (tutorial/snack), working title, where it goes, roughly how long.

## 2. Course fit (agent fills)

Which class module it supports, the relevant module learning outcomes (quoted from the course page), and where it sits in the arc (what comes before/after).

## 3. Sources (agent fills)

What this document builds on — lectures, readings, existing VisSnacks pages, papers. Explicitly noted: it is not 1-to-1 with a lecture.

## 4. High-value material in the slides (agent fills)

Slides that explicitly state takeaways/lessons/warnings, and particularly compelling examples — with slide numbers, so Mike doesn't have to dig through the whole deck.

## 5. What to cut / what must survive (Mike fills)

Which parts of the lecture are really in-class exercises or class-only business; which asides are load-bearing. Also: the ICE call for this page (skip / "try it" box / mention class).

## 6. The stories (Mike fills)

2–3 bullets of personal material: opinions, anecdotes, favorite examples, history. The decks don't carry these and the agent won't invent them.

## 7. Contested takes (Mike fills)

Places where Mike's view differs from the readings (Munzner, Cairo, the papers). These are the most valuable sentences on the site and the least recoverable from slides.

## 8. Anything else (Mike fills)

Constraints, connections, warnings, wishes.
